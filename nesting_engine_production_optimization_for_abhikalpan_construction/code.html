FROM PHYSICAL CUTOUTS TO MINKOWSKI EXPANSION

The No-Fit Polygon (NFP) Breakthrough

Standard collision detection evaluates overlaps after placing a polygon. For 200 parts, this causes an $O(N^2)$ explosion. The Nesting Engine instead generates the orbiting boundary—the No-Fit Polygon—once per geometry pair. Placing Part B along Part A's NFP perimeter guarantees 100% boundary contact with exactly 0.0mm of illegal overlap.

“Now let the computer run the geometric search.”

05 // GEOMETRIC RULES

A GOOD FIT STILL HAS TO FOLLOW THE RULES.

Pure mathematics will casually place two parts 0.1mm apart or overhang the boundary by a hair. In industrial production, that damages the equipment.

01

Hard Plate Boundaries

Zero tolerance for border overflow. Margins account for clamp zones and raw sheet edge warping from the rolling mill.

RULE: Δx_margin ≥ 12.0mm
02

Kerf & Plasma Heat Clearance

The plasma beam vaporizes steel at width $K$. If paths run too close, thermal distortion bends adjacent edges out of structural tolerance.

RULE: Kerf Offset = 3.8mm ± 0.2
03

Structural Grain Direction

Certain high-tensile cantilever brackets cannot be rotated arbitrarily. Their load path must align with the primary rolling grain of the plate.

RULE: Orientation Lock ∈ {0°, 180°}
04

Piercing Points & Lead-In Clearances

When the cutter initiates, molten blowback occurs. The lead-in piercing crater must occur inside the scrap negative space, never directly on a finished geometry edge.

RULE: Lead-in radius ≥ 6.5mm in dross pocket
05

Remnant Geometry Preservation

A 90% nesting that leaves a useless spiderweb of 20mm ribbons is worse than an 85% nesting that leaves a solid 600×1200mm pristine slab that can be inventoried for tomorrow's job.

RULE: Minimum Remnant Bounding Box ≥ 400x400mm
